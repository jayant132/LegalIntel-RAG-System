Group Records Retention Policy

Last reviewed: 14 February 2023 · Policy owner: Group Legal · Applies to: all Veridian entities

This policy defines how long records must be kept and when they must be destroyed. Retention periods run from the trigger event shown in the table. Where a longer period is required by local law, the longer period applies.

Record typeRetention periodTrigger
Executed commercial contracts and amendments7 yearsAfter expiry or termination
Employee personnel files6 yearsAfter employment ends
Payroll and tax records7 yearsEnd of the tax year
Board and committee minutesPermanent-
Business email (non-privileged)3 yearsDate sent
Marketing contact data2 yearsAfter last interaction
CCTV footage30 daysDate recorded

Legal hold

Where litigation, a regulatory investigation or an audit is reasonably anticipated, Group Legal will issue a Legal Hold notice. All destruction of the relevant records must be suspended immediately and until the hold is lifted in writing, regardless of the retention periods above.

Destruction

Records past their retention period must be securely destroyed within 90 days using approved methods. Destruction must be logged.
