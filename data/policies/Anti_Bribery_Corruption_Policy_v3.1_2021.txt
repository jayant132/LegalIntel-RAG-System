VERIDIAN GLOBAL INDUSTRIES PLC
ANTI-BRIBERY AND CORRUPTION POLICY   Version 3.1   (January 2021)

1. PURPOSE
This policy sets out our commitment to conducting business with integrity and applies to all employees and persons acting on our behalf.

2. CORE PROHIBITION
We do not offer or accept bribes. Facilitation payments are prohibited, except where an employee faces an imminent threat to personal health or safety; any such payment must be reported to Compliance within 24 hours.

3. GIFTS AND HOSPITALITY
3.1 Gifts with a value exceeding GBP 150 (or local equivalent) must be approved by the employee's line manager.
3.2 Hospitality exceeding GBP 250 per person must be approved by Group Compliance.
3.3 Gifts to government officials must be approved in advance by Group Compliance.
3.4 Cash may never be given or accepted.

4. THIRD PARTIES
Due diligence must be conducted on third-party intermediaries assessed as high risk.

5. TRAINING
Anti-bribery training is mandatory for all employees once every two years.

6. SPEAKING UP
Concerns can be raised with line managers, Group Compliance or the Speak-Up Line.

7. RECORDS
Records are retained for five (5) years.
